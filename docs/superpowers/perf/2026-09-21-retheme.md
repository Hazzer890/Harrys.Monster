Measured with the chrome-devtools MCP tools (`performance_start_trace` for LCP/CLS, `list_network_requests` for third-party count, `evaluate_script` for frame timing). Third-party requests are those to a host other than `localhost` (`fonts.googleapis.com`, `fonts.gstatic.com` x2, `analytics.harrys.monster`); the inline `data:` SVG noise texture has no host and isn't counted. After numbers are from the second of two runs per page (first run warms the browser); both runs are in the task 8 report.

Idle and scroll long-frame counts are at or near zero on both pages now, and LCP is well under the 2,500 ms target on both (1,408 ms on index, 1,556 ms on guide-to-github). CLS held at 0.00 on both, no worse than before. Third-party requests aren't at zero: the Google Fonts requests are gone, but one request to `analytics.harrys.monster` remains on each page, which is the site's own first-party analytics endpoint rather than a genuine third party. Index now transfers about 578 KB across 11 requests, most of it the headshot photo (389 KB) and the halftone coast SVG (112 KB).
