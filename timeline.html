About Me

Hi, I'm Harry. I can't remember a time I didn't want to change the world with engineering, and these days that looks like robots, data, and helping other people build both. I'm at my best helping someone else get to their best or chewing on a problem nobody has solved yet, and the good days are both at once. I've led a student robotics team for ten seasons, mentor a second one, and tutor about 80 students a semester in mechatronics labs.

I build software and hardware, a lot of it with AI in the loop these days, and I don't always know how to solve the problem in front of me, troubleshooting it is the fun part. Here's what I've been working with:

3D Design (Autodesk Suite, Solidworks, Onshape) C & C++ Python Microsoft Suite Microcontrollers Git Claude & OpenAI APIs Agentic systems TypeScript AI-assisted analytics
